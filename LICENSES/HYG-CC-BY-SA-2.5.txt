File: public/media/stars.csv; 43 HYG v2 stellar rows, with constellation headings.
Provenance: distributed with Spacescape by Alex Peterson and subsequently BinaryConstruct Skybox. The retained CSV matches Spacescape's stars.csv after line-ending normalization. All 43 data rows and the header match the archived HYG v2 hygxyz.csv exactly. No data edits were made during this publication audit. The subset/heading arrangement was inherited from Spacescape.
